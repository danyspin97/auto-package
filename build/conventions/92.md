92. **Every `_service` you add runs in manual mode.** Each `<service>` you write
   into `_service` (`download_files`, `cargo_vendor`, `node_modules`, ...) carries
   `mode="manual"`:
     <services>
       <service name="download_files" mode="manual"/>
     </services>
   The pipeline runs `osc service ra` before the build and commits what the
   services produced, so OBS never fetches anything at build time. Leave the
   services of a carried-forward recipe as they are (convention 86).
